# ADR 0001 — The session is 120 minutes

- Status: accepted, 2026-10-05
- Context: three of four blind v2 reviewers timed the beats and found the 90-minute box did not close (season 0 needs 8–10 minutes; 5 + 6×10 + 20 = 85 before role cards, hand-overs and surveys).
- Decision: 120 minutes — play ≤ 80 (time-box event), Lederman debrief 25, institution-design deliberation 15, logistics 15; consent and pre-survey before, review form and delayed post-test after. 90-minute fallback: time-box at 60, deliberation moved to the two-week review.
- Consequences: R20, §5.0 timetable, `session` block in scenario.json, `game.timeboxed` event, pilot gates.
