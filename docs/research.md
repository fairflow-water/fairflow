# Research design

The research design is blueprint §10 (`blueprint.md`). This page holds the links that must not live in the blueprint itself.

- **Pre-registration (OSF):** _to be added before the first approved cohort_. Exploratory hypotheses 1–6 of §10.3; one cohort is descriptive and supports no causal claim.
- **Ethics protocol:** submitted in build week 1; reference number _pending_. The first cohort is course-only unless approval arrives before it.
- **Measures:** pre-survey (own phone, before the session), immediate post (paper), delayed post (two-week review), joined by a participant-generated linking code. Instruments live in `../content/surveys/`.
- **Data:** per-cohort datasets, the frozen export schema and the codebook are released from `fairflow-water/fairflow-data`, never from this repository. Pilot records (`provenance.kind = "pilot"`) are in `packages/scenarios/pilots/`.
- **Publication plan:** JOSS paper on the engine at v1.1 (`../paper/`); serious-game paper after two cohorts.
